About

Four years of coursework, eight months of real data.

Most reporting problems are not a shortage of data. There is too much of it, in too many places. Sales in one sheet, ads in another, a third file somebody exported in March that nobody has opened since. The work is pulling those into one place, fixing what is broken inside them, and putting the result on a single page you can read without asking anyone what it means.

I did that in a primary care facility and in an education company. At La Castellana Primary Care Facility, as a volunteer, I consolidated 3,300+ emergency room records and built the facility's first interactive ER dashboard, plus a mortality rate dashboard to watch trends over time. At Kippap Learning Corporation I cleaned LMS and assessment data for 500+ students, resolved data quality issues across 10+ datasets, built 2 Power BI dashboards, set up a support tracker with real-time KPIs for 60+ cases a month, and sent instructors and stakeholders a weekly report.

Resume · Experience: La Castellana bullets 1–3, Kippap bullets 1–6.

Before either of those, my capstone integrated academic, payment and sentiment data and trained models to flag students at risk of dropping out. The degree underneath all of it is a BS in Information Technology, major in Data Analytics, from Mapúa Malayan Digital College, which I finished Summa Cum Laude.

The tools I work in are listed below, exactly as they appear on my resume. If your stack has something I have not used, I will say so instead of guessing.

How I work

From raw export to a decision

The same five steps sit under every project below.

  1. Consolidate

    Bring scattered exports into one dataset.

    3,300+ ER intake and treatment records, La Castellana

  2. Clean & validate

    Fix duplicates, gaps, types and outliers, then check the result adds up.

    Quality issues resolved across 10+ datasets, Kippap

  3. Analyze

    Define the KPIs, then measure, segment or model.

    Random Forest, XGBoost and Logistic Regression compared, capstone

  4. Visualize

    One dashboard people can filter without help.

    Power BI, Google Sheets, Apps Script and Tableau dashboards

  5. Report

    Deliver it on a schedule, with a note on what moved.

    Weekly Power BI reports to instructors and stakeholders, Kippap

Services

What you can hire me to do

Six things, each one drawn from work already done.

Dashboards that hold the whole picture

Sales, bookings, tickets, whatever you track, pulled into one page you can open on a Monday and read without asking anyone what it means. Built in Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio or plain Google Sheets, whichever you already pay for.

Proven by the first interactive dashboard La Castellana had, and 2 Power BI dashboards at Kippap

Spreadsheet cleanup and merge

Duplicate rows, three spellings of the same customer, dates in four formats, two exports that refuse to line up. I clean them, merge them, and give you back one file that adds up.

Proven by 10+ datasets with quality issues resolved at Kippap, and 500+ student records cleaned

Reports that arrive on schedule

A weekly or monthly report that lands without you chasing it, in the same shape every time, with a short written note on what moved and what did not.

Proven by weekly Power BI reports delivered to instructors and stakeholders at Kippap

KPI tracking, set up from scratch

We pick the handful of numbers that actually decide your week. I set up the tracking, the definitions and the refresh, so those numbers stay live instead of being rebuilt by hand every time somebody asks.

Proven by a support tracker with real-time KPIs at Kippap, 60+ cases a month with root causes

SQL and database work

Data that has outgrown a spreadsheet gets tables, keys and queries instead of another tab. Joining sources that were never designed to talk to each other is usually the first job.

Proven by 500+ student records cleaned in MySQL Workbench, and academic, payment and sentiment data integrated in Microsoft Access

Risk scoring from your own history

If you have a record of who stayed and who left, that record can score who is at risk now. Trained, measured and user-tested before anyone acts on it.

Proven by the capstone churn model at 90.26% accuracy and 86.97% F1, validated in UAT
