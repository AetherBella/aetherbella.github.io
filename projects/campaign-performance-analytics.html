Campaign performance analytics

A public dataset of 10,000 digital ad campaigns ran 41 columns wide. I connected it in Google Sheets, added profit, margin and ROAS calculations, and built a Google Apps Script web app on top: five pages and nine filters, with every KPI recalculating as the filters change.

01Business problem

A marketing team running campaigns across several platforms wants one answer above all: where does each dollar of ad spend come back largest? In a flat sheet of 10,000 rows and 41 columns, that answer is buried. Comparing platforms, objectives, audiences and creatives meant filtering and pivoting by hand every time.

02Objectives

  • Track campaign performance, audience behavior and profitability in one shareable dashboard.
  • Show which platforms and objectives return the most revenue per dollar spent.
  • Break results down by audience (device type, target gender) and creative.
  • Let anyone filter and review results without working in the spreadsheet.

04Data preparation

  1. LoadThe 10,000-row, 41-column dataset connected in Google Sheets as the single source.
  2. DeriveProfit, margin and ROAS calculations added alongside the raw measures.
  3. ServeGoogle Apps Script reads the sheet and serves it as a web app.
  4. RefreshThe dashboard refreshes from the source sheet, so edits flow through.

05Analytical approach

Each KPI card follows a standard definition. Each was checked against the dashboard's own totals.

CTR
Clicks ÷ impressions = 2.16%
Avg CPC
Ad spend ÷ clicks = $2.85
Conversion rate
Conversions ÷ clicks = 4.30%
CPA
Ad spend ÷ conversions = $66
Profit
Revenue − ad spend = $240.70M
Margin
Profit ÷ revenue = 84.71%
ROAS
Revenue ÷ ad spend = 6.54x
Revenue per campaign
Revenue ÷ campaigns = $28,416

Nine filters (platform, objective, industry, device type, target gender, budget tier, year, quarter and month) apply to every page. The pages are Overview, Campaign Performance, Audience, Creative Analysis and Insights.

06Dashboard & findings

  • Strong overall return. $43.46M of ad spend returned $284.16M of revenue: a 6.54x ROAS and an 84.71% margin.
  • The funnel narrows fast. 705.4M impressions became 15.3M clicks (2.16% CTR) and 655,688 conversions (4.30% of clicks).
  • Campaign mix is concentrated. Google Ads ran 29.3% of campaigns and Facebook 25.3%, ahead of LinkedIn (14.7%), TikTok (12.1%), Twitter (10.3%) and Instagram (8.3%).

The full-width embed fills the content column. The app itself sets its own internal layout, which is designed for desktop widths; on phones, "Open the live dashboard" gives the best view.

07Business impact

Verified outcomes

  • A browser-based dashboard that refreshes from the source sheet.
  • Anyone with the link can filter and review campaign results without opening or editing the spreadsheet.

Potential benefits

  • Shifting budget toward the platforms and objectives with the highest ROAS.
  • Spotting audience segments or creatives that cost more per conversion.
