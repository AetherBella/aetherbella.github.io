Case study 02 · Education

Student performance & passing-rate dashboards

An education company's LMS and assessment data for 500+ students was spread across datasets with quality problems. I cleaned it with SQL and Excel, built two Power BI dashboards, including one ranking board-exam passing rates by study technique and device, and sent instructors and stakeholders a weekly report. Alongside it, I built a support tracker with real-time KPIs.

01Business problem

LMS and assessment data for more than 500 students sat across datasets carrying quality problems, which held back the accuracy and consistency of reporting. Instructors and stakeholders needed a dependable view of engagement, passing rates and performance trends.

Separately, support cases were being worked without a running record of their volume or cause.

02Objectives

  • Which study techniques and devices go with higher board-exam passing rates?
  • How do results look across working status, age and first-time takers?
  • How are student engagement and performance trending?
  • For support: what is the case volume, how do inquiries and issues split, how long does resolution take, and what are the root causes by category, channel and weekday?

04Data preparation

  1. QueryLMS and assessment data pulled and analyzed with SQL in MySQL Workbench, plus Excel/CSV files.
  2. AuditData quality issues identified dataset by dataset across 10+ datasets.
  3. ResolveIssues fixed so reporting would be accurate and consistent.
  4. Model in Power BIClean data feeds the dashboards and the weekly report.

05Analytical approach

  • Passing-rate ranking. Board-exam passing rate ranked by study technique and by device.
  • Segment filters. Working status, age and first-time takers, so the ranking can be read for each group.
  • Engagement and performance trends across the two Power BI dashboards.
  • Support KPIs. Case volume, inquiry rate, issue rate, resolution time and backlog, with root causes ranked by category, channel and weekday.

06Dashboards

The support tracker below is built in Google Sheets on the tracker's own case log. The Power BI passing-rate dashboard is not published, because it is built on student data.

07Business impact

Verified outcomes

  • Data quality issues resolved across 10+ datasets, improving the accuracy and consistency of reporting.
  • Weekly Power BI reports and insights delivered to instructors and stakeholders.
  • A support tracker with real-time KPIs for 60+ cases a month, where no running record existed.

Potential benefits

  • Recommending the study techniques and devices linked to higher passing rates.
  • Targeting the most frequent support root causes first.
